Marquee for Mac
===============

Installing
----------
Drag Marquee onto the Applications folder in this window. Then open it from
Applications (or Launchpad, or Spotlight).

Opening it the first time
-------------------------
Marquee isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time you open it macOS
says it can't check it for malicious software. Choose Done, then:

  1. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security.
  2. Scroll down to the message about Marquee and click Open Anyway.
  3. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

That's once per install.

Connecting
----------
Marquee is the app for your own Marquee server. On first launch it looks for
servers on your network; pick yours (or type its address, e.g.
192.168.1.20:3000) and sign in with your Marquee account.

Updating
--------
You don't need to download new versions yourself. Marquee checks for updates
when it opens and once a day; when one is out, an update button appears on
the left-hand rail. Settings › About (and Marquee › Check for Updates…) has
Update too: Marquee downloads the new version, checks it, then quits and
reopens on it.

Every download is named for its version (e.g. Marquee-0.31.0.dmg), so the
newest one in your Downloads folder is the one with the highest number.
